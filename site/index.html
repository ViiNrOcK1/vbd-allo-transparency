Where does the allocation go?

Every VeBetterDAO round, B3TR is allocated to apps. Part goes to the app's rewards pool for its users, part to the team wallet. This page follows both, straight from VeChain mainnet.

How to read this
  • Allocated — B3TR the app claimed from VeBetterDAO's allocation rounds (AllocationRewardsClaimed).
  • Taken by team — the team's official share of each allocation plus what it withdrew from its own rewards pool, as a % of everything allocated. Most apps take 0% as a share and withdraw instead, so the share alone would hide it. Withdrawing isn't wrong in itself (each withdrawal records a reason on-chain); this just shows the size.
  • Paid to users — exact: B3TR can only leave an app's rewards pool by being paid to a user or withdrawn by the team, so paid = deposited − withdrawn − still in the pool.
  • Sold — B3TR sent from a team wallet in a transaction that swapped in a pool holding B3TR. Adding liquidity is counted separately and is not a sale.
  • Other wallets / contracts — transfers we can't identify. They are not assumed to be sales; the top recipients are listed so you can check them yourself.
  • When several apps share one team wallet, its flows can't be split between them; those apps are marked shared wallet.

Not financial advice and not an accusation of anyone. Team wallets are allowed to spend their share; this only shows what happened.
